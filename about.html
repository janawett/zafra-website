About Zafra

One Ingredient. One Lagoon. One Tradition.

About

Zafra is a premium Mexican sea salt harvested by hand from Laguna de Cuyutlán, Colima — one of Mexico's oldest salt-producing lagoons. The salt is naturally filtered through ancient clay beds and sun-dried without additives or processing. An independent Mexican brand built around one ingredient, one lagoon, and one unbroken tradition.

FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions

Where does Zafra sea salt come from?

Zafra sea salt is harvested by hand from Laguna de Cuyutlán, a coastal lagoon in Colima, Mexico — one of the country's oldest and most storied salt-producing regions.

How is Zafra salt made?

Zafra salt is solar-evaporated and hand-harvested with no mechanical processing or additives. The lagoon's natural clay beds act as a filtration layer, contributing to the salt's mineral profile and purity.

Is Zafra salt pure and additive-free?

Yes. Zafra contains no anti-caking agents, iodine additives, or artificial processing of any kind. It is harvested and dried naturally, then packaged without further treatment.

What makes Zafra different from other sea salts?

Zafra is single-origin, artisan-harvested, and traceable to a specific lagoon with a documented production tradition. Unlike commodity sea salts, it is not blended, refined, or sourced from multiple locations.

Who is Zafra for?

Zafra is for home cooks, professional chefs, and specialty food buyers who care about ingredient origin and quality. It is also well suited for gifting and retail contexts where provenance and craft are part of the value.

Where can I buy Zafra sea salt?

Zafra is available for purchase on Amazon. Wholesale and retail inquiries can be directed to hello@zafrasalt.com.

Is Zafra sea salt tested for quality and safety?

Yes. Zafra undergoes third-party laboratory testing including heavy metals analysis to verify purity and safety standards. Full results are published at zafrasalt.com/testing.
